Your account

Sign in and CraveCast keeps your habits, logs and garden on the server instead of only in this browser — so they survive clearing your history and follow you to any device you sign in on. Everything already in this browser is merged in, never replaced.
🌱
Delete this account
Removes the login and every habit, log and plant stored on the server, permanently. Anything still in this browser is left alone — use Erase everything at the foot of the page for that.

Pick something to grow

One clean day grows one thing. Plant it, get through 24 hours without logging that you gave in, and it moves into your garden for good. Log a slip before the time is up and that one is lost — but nothing you have already grown is ever taken away.

Tracked habits

Each habit gets its own forecast. Deleting one deletes its logs.
